Official-source and blank-value policy

Every data-table row links to its official MLB response. Scores, counts, inning totals, player statistics and challenge counters are rendered only if MLB supplies the field. Zero is displayed only when MLB explicitly reports zero. A shaded empty cell means unreported/unverified, not zero. An unplayed half-inning is also left blank.

Source links open the current official response, which MLB may revise. An observation log records what this browser saw, not a certified historical archive. Derived interface counts count observed records; they do not claim complete day totals.
